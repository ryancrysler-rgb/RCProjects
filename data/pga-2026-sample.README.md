# Winners vs field shot sample, 2026 (rebuilt 19 Nov 2026)

Rebuild of the lost 30–31 Oct sample (owner "Go, rebuild the original sample"): at every PGA TOUR stroke-play event with shot data from
The American Express through the Butterfield Bermuda Championship (37 events; the Masters and The Open have no shot data; Zurich and the
Presidents Cup are team events), the winner + 10 players picked evenly from 2nd to the missed cut (WD/DQ left out), every round they played.
Per event: leaderboard.json, cs<round>.json (field hole averages per round), raw/<playerId>-<round>.json (get_player_shots_compact).
sample-plan.json lists who was picked. Rounds already held in ../pga-2026 are not duplicated here (see sample-plan "need").
Private, internal (pgatour.com via our Parse.bot build 7f7abae7).
