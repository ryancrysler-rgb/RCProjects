THIS MONTH'S FOCUS

Skills under pressure

01Build
02Transfer
03Compete
04Perform

With individual blueprints established, October's focus shifted to testing how well students' skills held up under pressure as the competitive season began. Sessions featured changing targets, lies and clubs, with a strong emphasis on one-ball decision-making:

One ball→One decision→Consequences→Emotional response→Reset→Next shot

This approach challenged students to commit to each shot, accept the outcome and manage their response before preparing for the next. Weekly games on the course and at the academy showed where each student's skills sat within the Build – Transfer – Compete – Perform framework, and what needed more work to carry into competition. Combined with Clippd data, these observations guided sessions with each student's coach, so practice focused on what would make the biggest difference in competition.

ELITE · SWITZERLAND

Kiril Poretti

Swiss U16 ChampionItalian U16 ChampionSwiss U18 runner-up

A fantastic summer saw new Elite student Kiril Poretti win the Swiss and Italian Under-16 Championships and finish runner-up in the Swiss Under-18 Championship, earning a call-up to the Swiss National Team against Belgium, Holland and Italy.

Kiril contributed 2.5 valuable points, winning his singles matches against Holland and Italy and halving his foursomes match against Belgium. Switzerland narrowly missed out on overall victory, but it was fantastic to see Kiril represent his country and make such a positive contribution.

OCTOBER HALF-TERM · THE ELS CLUB AND EARTH COURSE

Six days of WAGR golf

October half-term brought the first major test of the season for our elite students. A three-day WAGR ProDream event at The Els Club was followed immediately by another three-day WAGR event on the Earth Course, part of the TFA Series. Six days of competition in a row, in humid conditions, made for a gruelling test, with fantastic golf played throughout.

Victor Larsson, Aodhan Fitzgerald and Lewis Salthouse finished tied seventh in the ProDream event, all at three under par.

Victor continued his impressive start to the season with rounds of 69, 68 and 67 in the TFA Series, finishing 12 under par to take second place and move to 1,794th in the WAGR.

Max Rogers and Daniel Chaney

AL ZORAH GOLF CLUB · 23–25 OCTOBER

The inaugural EGF Trophy

Al Zorah Golf Club hosted the first EGF Trophy, a three-day WAGR event that drew some of the region's best golfers and produced impressive scoring throughout.

Aodhan Fitzgerald won the Under-13 division with rounds of 73, 71 and 72 for a level-par total. Lewis Salthouse took second in the Under-15 division, closing with an excellent 67 after opening rounds of 73 and 73 for a three-under-par total.
