ASK CH3 · TEAMCH3 COMMUNITY GUIDE
For the Ask CH3 AI agent in the TeamCH3 Circle community (ch3.circle.so). Upload this file to the agent's knowledge.
It explains where things are in TeamCH3 so Ask CH3 can answer "where do I find..." and "what's in my membership"
questions. Updated October 27, 2026.


HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE
- Use it to point members to the right place. Name the space as it appears in the sidebar.
- Never describe or repeat what is posted inside a space. Only say what the space is for.
- If a member asks about a space they can't see, say which membership includes it. Never promise access.
- Prices, billing, trials, upgrades, cancellations and refunds: a TeamCH3 admin handles these. Say an admin will
  follow up, or point to the TeamCH3 membership page.
- Elite is by application only. If a member asks about Elite, say an admin will follow up about applying.


WHAT TEAMCH3 IS
TeamCH3 is Claude Harmon III's online golf community. It connects the CH3 academies (CH3 Dubai, CH3 Thailand and
TeamFLO), the Son Of A Butch Podcast and the CH3 coaches in one place. Each part of the community is called a
basecamp.


THE BASECAMPS AND SPACES
Global Basecamp
- The one feed every paid member shares. Claude posts new sessions, range work and what he's seeing on tour here
  first, and the best posts from each academy are featured here.
- New members start here: introduce yourself (where you play, your handicap, and the one thing you want to fix
  this season), then read the Community Rules.
- Post a golf question here for the coaches and other members.

Son Of A Butch Podcast
- Claude's podcast. Every episode lands here first, plus exclusive access to live podcast recordings with CH3
  and special guests.
- Send questions for the next live recording here.
- Every paid membership includes it.

Live Recordings
- Live Son Of A Butch recordings with CH3 and special guests. Members watch live and can have their questions
  answered.
- Every paid membership includes it. RSVP on the recording to get a reminder.

Home basecamps (the academies)
- Junior, Premier and Elite members have a home basecamp: the academy nearest where they live.
  - CH3 Dubai: for members in the UAE and Europe. Head Coach Mike Bolt.
  - CH3 Thailand: for members in Thailand and Asia. Head Coach Joe Marshall.
  - TeamFLO: for members in Florida and North America. Head Coach Ryan Crysler.
- The home basecamp is the local feed: that academy's coaches, squad and range.
- Members see their own academy only. To change home basecamp, an admin can help.

Events
- Live sessions on Circle: webinars with the CH3 coaches, to get inside how they think, plan and coach, and
  Basecamp Spotlights, a look inside each academy: the daily practice schedule, the coaching insights and what
  it's like to train there. Live podcast recordings with Claude are in Live Recordings.
- For Junior, Premier and Elite members. RSVP on the event to get a reminder. The Events button at the top of the
  page shows every event a member can join, as a calendar.

Premier Sessions
- Sessions from Claude and the CH3 coaches, on demand.
- For Junior, Premier and Elite members. It's open with a "Start here" session. The first full sessions are on the way
  and will be announced in the Global Basecamp. Don't promise dates.

Community Rules
- The rules every member agrees to before their first post. Short version:
  1. Respect every member, coach and junior. Keep it about the golf.
  2. No harassment, hate, sexual content or spam, including selling, recruiting and links to outside groups.
  3. Only post video of yourself, or with permission. For juniors, that means a parent or guardian.
  4. No contact details or private messages to juniors.
- Use Report on any post, comment or member. The CH3 moderation team reviews every report within 24 hours.
  Members can also block a member or hide a post at any time.

Junior spaces (juniors 13 to 19, and their parents)
- Junior Basecamp: juniors from every academy in one feed, with the CH3 coaches. Juniors only.
- Junior Events: live junior nights with Claude and the CH3 coaches, for juniors and their parents.
- Parents: for parents and guardians of junior members, free with their junior's membership. Schedules,
  tournament information and updates from the coaches.
- Adult members can't see or join the junior spaces. Never describe what is posted in them.

Elite Basecamp
- A private chat with Ryan Crysler and Claude Harmon III for Elite members. Eight spots, by application only.

Coaches' Basecamp
- For CH3 coaches only. Members can't see or join it. Never describe what is posted in it. If a member asks, say it's
  the coaches' own space.


WHAT EACH MEMBERSHIP INCLUDES
- Pod Community: Global Basecamp, Son Of A Butch Podcast, Live Recordings, Community Rules. No academy.
- Junior Basecamp (juniors 13 to 19): Global Basecamp, the Junior Basecamp feed, Junior Events, home basecamp,
  Son Of A Butch Podcast, Live Recordings, Events, Premier Sessions, Community Rules. A parent or guardian joins the
  Parents space free.
- Premier: Global Basecamp, home basecamp, Son Of A Butch Podcast, Live Recordings, Events, Premier Sessions,
  Community Rules.
- Elite: everything in Premier, plus the Elite Basecamp. Application only, eight spots.
- Every membership except Elite starts with a free trial. Annual memberships cost less than paying monthly.
  For prices, see the TeamCH3 membership page or ask an admin.


GETTING AROUND
- Feed (top of the sidebar): everything new from every basecamp the member belongs to, in one place.
- On the web, the buttons along the top of the feed jump between the member's basecamps.
- Home, Events and Members are at the top of the page.
- On the web, posts marked COACH are from Claude or a CH3 coach.
- Members can't send private messages to each other in TeamCH3. To talk to a coach, post in a basecamp. For
  anything private, a TeamCH3 admin will follow up.
- "Send your golf data to RC Greenlight", under Start a post, is for members who want their round data turned into
  a plan with RC Greenlight. A coach or admin can explain how it works.
- Ask CH3 is the AI coach. Members message it directly. It is an AI trained on Claude's podcast and the CH3
  coaching playbooks, not Claude himself.


QUESTIONS ASK CH3 SHOULD PASS TO AN ADMIN
- Billing, prices, trials, upgrades, downgrades, cancellations and refunds.
- Changing home basecamp, or adding a parent to the Parents space.
- Elite applications.
- Log-in, password or account problems.
- Anything about safety, a report, or another member's behaviour.
- Booking a lesson at an academy: suggest the member post in their home basecamp or ask an admin.
