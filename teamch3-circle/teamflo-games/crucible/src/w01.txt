Crucible Blueprint: Week 1 - Foundational Control
Mission: Master the fundamentals of control. Establish a baseline of precision in putting, chipping, and iron play before earning the right to hit for power.
1. Short Game: Putting Start Line
   * HIIT: High Knees in Place (60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The Putt Gate. Set up a 4-foot putt. Place two tees just wider than your putter head to form a gate. You must make 10 consecutive putts without the putter touching the gate.
   * Consequence: Hitting a tee or missing a putt resets your count to zero.
2. Full Swing: Iron Strike
   * HIIT: Squat Jumps (45s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Center-Face Purity. Spray your 7-iron face. You must produce 5 consecutive centered strikes (a clean circle in the middle).
   * Consequence: An off-center strike (heel or toe) resets your count to zero.
3. Visualization: The Opening Tee Shot
   * HIIT: Fast Feet / Ickey Shuffle (60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Stand behind your "practice" tee. Close your eyes and execute a full, vivid pre-shot routine in your mind. See the target, feel the swing, and visualize the perfect ball flight finding the fairway.
   * Consequence: If your mind wanders or you lose focus, you must restart the visualization. Perform this 3 consecutive times with perfect mental clarity.
4. Short Game: Chipping Landing Zone
   * HIIT: Alternating Lunges (60s)
   * "Do or die" Drill: The Towel Drop. Place a towel 10-15 yards away. You have 5 balls to land 3 of them on the towel.
   * Consequence: Failure to land 3/5 on the towel means you perform a 30-second plank before restarting the drill.
5. Full Swing: Fairway Finder
   * HIIT: Burpees (45s)
   * "Do or die" Drill: The 30-Yard Channel. With your longest iron, hit to a target. Your shot must stop within a 3 finger channel. You must hit 3 consecutive shots in the channel.
   * Consequence: One shot outside the channel resets your count to zero.
6. Visualization: The Pressure Putt
   * HIIT: Mountain Climbers (60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Stand over a 10-foot putt. Close your eyes. Visualize making the putt to win your Club Championship. See the line, feel the stroke, hear the ball drop in the cup.
   * Consequence: You must hold this positive visualization for 30 uninterrupted seconds. If negative thoughts intrude, restart. Perform 3 times.
