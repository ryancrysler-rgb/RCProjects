Crucible Blueprint: Week 12 - Final Exam: The Grinder
Mission: To combine all elements of Month 3 into a final test of resilience. This is a non-stop gauntlet designed to simulate the final, grueling holes of a tournament.
1. Short Game: The 18th Hole Bunker
   * HIIT: Prowler Sled Push or Car Push (45s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: You are short-sided in a bunker on the final hole. You must get up-and-down to win. You get one ball.
   * Consequence: Pass or Fail. Failure means you have "lost the tournament." Your consequence is to re-do the HIIT push, then come back and successfully get up-and-down 2 times in a row from the 
   * same spot.
2. Full Swing: The Approach Shot
   * HIIT: Dumbbell Man-Makers (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Following your "successful" bunker shot, you now have 160 yards from the fairway. The pin is tucked. You must hit the green.
   * Consequence: Hitting the green is a pass. Missing the green is a fail. The consequence for failure is 10 burpees. You must succeed before moving on.
3. Visualization: The Winning Putt
   * HIIT: Controlled Breathing / Heart Rate Lowering (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: You've hit the green, leaving an 8-foot putt to win. Visualize the entire scene: the pressure, your opponent watching, the line of the putt. Mentally execute your full pre-shot routine and feel yourself stroke the putt into the center of the cup.  Make the putt.
   * Consequence: You must hold the feeling of confidence and commitment, not hope. Perform this 3 times with unwavering focus. 
4. Short Game: The Par Saver
   * HIIT: Wall Sit (hold until failure).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Your approach shot missed the green. You must now chip to within 3 feet of the hole to save par.
   * Consequence: You must execute 3 successful "par saver" chips in a row. A single chip finishing outside 3 feet resets the count to zero.
5. Full Swing: The Final Tee Shot
   * HIIT: Max height Vertical Jumps (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: It's the final hole. You need a fairway hit. This is a repeat of the "Must-Hit" fairway drill. You get one ball. You must find the fairway.
   * Consequence: Pass or Fail. Failure means you have not successfully completed the final exam. You must re-do the entire Week 12 blueprint on your next practice day.
6. Visualization: The Champion's Mindset
   * HIIT: Rest.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: You have successfully completed the gauntlet. Close your eyes and visualize the feeling of deep satisfaction that comes not from winning, but from knowing your discipline held up under extreme pressure. Acknowledge that the hard work and adherence to the process is the true victory.
   * Consequence: This is your reward. Hold this feeling of earned satisfaction. This solidifies the "why" behind the entire month of grueling work.
