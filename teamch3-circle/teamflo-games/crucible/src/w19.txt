Crucible Blueprint: Week 19 - Mastering Curves
Mission: To learn how to intentionally curve the ball on command. 
1. Short Game: The Cut Chip
   * HIIT: Explosive Medicine Ball Chest Passes (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Set up an alignment stick a few feet in front of you, to the left of the hole. You must hit a chip that starts left of the stick and curves (cuts) back towards the hole. This is a high-spin shot.
   * Consequence: You must get 4 out of 5 shots to start left, curve right, and finish within 6 feet of the hole. Failure means 10 mountain climbers before restarting.
2. Full Swing: The Fade Gate
   * HIIT: Dumbbell Push Press (60s). Build shoulder stability to control the "out-to-in" path.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Set up a "gate" with two alignment sticks or headcovers about 4 feet in front of you and slightly to the left of your target line. With a 7-iron, your ball must start through the gate and fade back to the target.
   * Consequence: You must hit 3 consecutive shots through the gate that fade back into a 30-yard wide fairway. A single pull or straight shot resets the count.
3. Visualization: The Left-Tucked Pin (DRAW)
   * HIIT: Plank with alternating shoulder taps (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize an approach shot to a green with a pin tucked on the left side, guarded by water. Visualize aiming for the center of the green and hitting a high, soft draw that drifts towards the flag, landing with control.
   * Consequence: This is  a precision shot. Your visualization must feel controlled and deliberate. You must feel confident in the shot shape. Perform this 10 times.
4. Short Game: The High-Spinning Pitch
   * HIIT: Broad Jumps (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From 40 yards to a tight pin, you must hit a high, spinning pitch shot that lands and stops quickly. The fade is the natural shot for this.
   * Consequence: You must execute 4 out of 5 shots that stop within 6 feet of the hole. Shots that release too far are failures. Failure requires a 30-second wall sit.
5. Full Swing: The "Fairway Finder" Fade
   * HIIT: Burpees (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The fade is often called the most controllable driver shot. You must hit 4 out of 5 drives that start down the left side of the fairway and gently curve back into the fairway.
   * Consequence: This is a test of control, not power. A hook or a slice that misses the fairway is a failure. Failure to hit 4/5 requires re-doing the HIIT workout.
6. Visualization: The Crosswind Tamer
   * HIIT: Bicycle Crunches (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize a crosswind. Instead of fighting it, visualize using it. Aim down the corresponding side of the fairway and hit a controlled curve that lets the wind keep it straight to the center.
   * Consequence: The visualization must be about working with the elements, not against them. You must feel a sense of strategic mastery. Perform this 10 times.
