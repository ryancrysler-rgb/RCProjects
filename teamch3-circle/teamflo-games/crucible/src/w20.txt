Crucible Blueprint: Week 20 - Final Exam: The Shot Maker
Mission: To synthesize all shot-shaping and adversity skills into a final test. You must be able to call your shot and execute under pressure and in simulated bad conditions.
1. Short Game: The On-Demand Chip
   * HIIT: Man-Makers (60s). A total-body test of grit.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Have a partner call out the shot: "low runner," "high spinner," "hook chip," "cut chip." You must execute the correct shot on command.
   * Consequence: You must successfully execute 4 out of 5 called shots. A single failure to produce the correct trajectory or shape is a miss. Failure to reach 4/5 means restarting the drill.
2. Full Swing: Call Your Curve
   * HIIT: Max reps Bodyweight Squats (60s).
   * "Do or die" Drill: You must hit a 3-shot sequence with a 5-iron: a Draw, a Fade, and a Straight shot. All three must land in a 30-yard wide fairway.
   * Consequence: The sequence must be perfect. A single failure on any of the three shots (wrong shape or missed fairway) requires you to perform 10 burpees and restart the entire 3-shot sequence.
3. Visualization: The "Worst-Case" Weather
   * HIIT: Controlled Breathing / Heart Rate Lowering (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize a worst-case scenario: 25-mph winds, driving rain, and cold. Mentally, you must accept the challenge with a positive mindset. Your visualization is to simply complete one hole, focusing on a smooth tempo, smart clubbing, and keeping a good attitude.
   * Consequence: Your goal is to visualize surviving the hole with a "good bogey" and feeling proud of your resilience. You must complete the visualization without letting the "weather" break you mentally.
4. Short Game: The "Impossible Lie" Escape
   * HIIT: Wall Sit (hold until failure).
   * "Do or die" Drill: Find a terrible lie near the green (in a divot, on hardpan, ball against the collar). You must get the ball on the green.
   * Consequence: This is not about getting it close; it's about a successful escape. You must succeed 3 times in a row from 3 different "impossible" lies. Failure to get the ball on the green resets your count to zero.
5. Full Swing: The Wind Gauntlet
   * HIIT: Sprint 50 yards, jog back (repeat for 90s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Hit a 3-shot sequence with your driver: a low stinger into a "headwind," a high shot with a "tailwind," and a controlled fade to "hold up" against a crosswind.
   * Consequence: You must successfully produce three visibly different trajectories that all find the fairway. A single failure means you have failed the exam. Your consequence is to re-do the entire Week 20 blueprint on your next practice day.
