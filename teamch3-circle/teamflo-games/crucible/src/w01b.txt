Crucible Blueprint Week 1(B)
Mission: A high-intensity circuit designed to expose weaknesses in your physical readiness, technical execution, and mental discipline. Complete each station in order. Your answers to the questions are as important as the results of the drills.
________________


1. The Routine: Your Anchor in Chaos
* The Exercise: Plank (Hold for 90 seconds). If you drop, you fail. This builds the core stability and mental endurance to stay disciplined when you are tired.
* The Task (Routine): Take one golf ball. Go through your full pre-shot routine for an imaginary 150-yard shot. Time it. It should take between 15-30 seconds. Repeat this 5 times. Your time for each routine must be within a 3-second window of the others (e.g., all routines between 20-23 seconds).
* The Question: Did you commit 100% to your decision before you swung, or were you second-guessing over the ball, allowing doubt to poison your process?
________________


2. The Putt: Where Scores Are Finalized
* The Exercise: Max Repetition Burpees (60 seconds). This elevates your heart rate and simulates the adrenaline of a must-make putt to win a match.
* The Task (Putting): The Circle of Trust. Place 6 balls in a circle, each 5 feet from the hole. You must make all 6 consecutively.
* The Question: After a miss, does your frustration contaminate the next stroke, or do you have a disciplined process to immediately let it go and focus on the "next shot" mentality?
________________


3. The Chip: The Scramble for Survival
* The Exercise: Max Repetition Bodyweight Squat Jumps (60 seconds). This builds the explosive leg power and stability needed to execute touch shots from imperfect lies.
* The Task (Chipping): The Up & Down Gauntlet. From a challenging lie in the rough 20 yards from the pin, you get one chip and one putt to get up and down. You must succeed 4 out of 6 times.
* The Question: Do you see a tough chip as a problem to be feared or as an opportunity to display your skill and resilience?
________________


4. The Iron: The Engine of Consistency
* The Exercise: Wall Sit (Hold until failure). This is a pure test of grit and your ability to tolerate sustained discomfort—a key attribute for grinding out a good score.
* The Task (Irons): The Greenlight Gauntlet. Pick a target 150 yards away and define a 15-yard wide "greenlight" zone. You must hit 3 consecutive shots that finish inside that zone. One miss resets the count to zero.
* The Question: Are you judging your performance by the quality of your process—the routine, the balance, the strike—or are you emotionally attached to the unpredictable outcome of the ball flight?
________________


5. The Drive: The Foundation of the Hole
* The Exercise: Explosive Broad Jumps (60 seconds). This builds the raw, horizontal power needed for the driver.
* The Task (Driving): The "One Shot" Qualifier. Place a single ball on the tee. Define a 30-yard wide fairway. This is the 18th hole of a U.S. Open qualifier. You get one shot. You must find the fairway. You must succeed 3 times in a row (resetting after each attempt).
* The Question: Do you take Extreme Ownership for a bad drive by analyzing your own failure in planning or execution, or do you immediately look for an external excuse?
