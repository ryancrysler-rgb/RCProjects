Mission: To challenge the consistency of your fundamentals. This week, we introduce drills that require you to maintain precision over longer sequences and varied conditions, cementing the control you built in weeks 1-3.
1. Short Game: Putting Gates & Speed
   * OPTIONAL HIIT: Jump Rope with High Knees (60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The Gauntlet. Place a "gate" 2 feet in front of your ball on a 15-foot putt. You must send the ball through the gate and have it finish within a 1-foot circle around the hole.
   * Consequence: You must succeed 5 times in a row. A single failure (hitting the gate or finishing outside the circle) resets your count to zero.
2. Full Swing: Shot Trajectory Control
   * OPTIONAL HIIT: Kettlebell Goblet Squats (45s) (Safe weight)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: High-Low Challenge. With a 6-iron, you must hit two consecutive shots to a target window. The first must be a high shot that lands softly; the second must be a low, piercing "stinger" shot. Both must land within a 20-yard wide fairway.
   * Consequence: You must execute 3 successful high-low pairs. If either shot in a pair misses the target window, the pair is a failure. Perform 5 broad jumps and restart the pair.
3. Visualization: Course Management Under Pressure
   * OPTIONAL HIIT: Plank with alternating shoulder taps (60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize a 420-yard par 4 with water down the right side. Verbally (or mentally) walk through your entire strategic process: identify the ideal leave yardage, select a conservative target on the opposite side of the trouble, and choose a club off the tee that maximizes your chance of hitting that target. Choose a club for the second shot and repeat.
   * Consequence: Your visualized plan must be decisive and logical. You must perform this visualization process 3 times, each time for a different challenging hole, without wavering or second-guessing your smart decision.
4. Short Game: The Scrambler's Test
   * OPTIONAL HIIT: Alternating Step-Ups onto a bench (60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The Up, Down, and In. From 25 yards off the green, you must get up and down in exactly two shots (chip and putt).
   * Consequence: You must succeed 3 times in a row from 3 different locations. A failure to get up and down in two shots resets your count to zero and you must move to a new location.
5. Full Swing: Variable Distance Irons
   * OPTIONAL HIIT: Dumbbell Push Press (45s) (Safe Weight or standing band press)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The Randomizer. Have a partner call out a random number or use the Google Random Number Generator for yardages between 120 and 170 yards. You have 15 seconds (set alarm) to select your club, take one practice swing, and hit the shot. The ball must land within 10% of the distance to the hole (e.g., within 15 yards on a 150-yard shot).
   * Consequence: You must execute 4 out of 5 successful shots. Failure requires a 30-second wall sit before restarting the 5-shot sequence.
6. Visualization: The "Bad Round" Reset
   * OPTIONAL HIIT: Bicycle Crunches (60s) (Lying on back doing crunches and pedaling your feet)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize being 4-over-par through 3 holes. Feel the frustration. Now, engage your "24-second rule" mental process. Take a deep breath, "throw away" the past holes, and re-focus on the process for the 4th tee shot—your stance, your target, your swing thought—not the score. Send it.
   * Consequence: You must achieve a mental state of neutral, process-focused commitment within 30 seconds. Repeat this visualization 3 times.
