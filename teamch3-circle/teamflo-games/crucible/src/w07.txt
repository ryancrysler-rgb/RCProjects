Crucible Blueprint: Week 7 - Managing Speed & Misses
Mission: To understand that with greater speed comes the potential for bigger misses. This week is about learning to control your top-end speed and manage the consequences.
1. Short Game: The Delicate Flop
   * HIIT: Rapid-fire Bodyweight Squats (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The Short-Side Stop. Place a ball on a tight lie, short-sided to a pin. You have to hit a high, soft flop shot that lands and stops within one flagstick of the hole. This requires speed, but with ultimate control.
   * Consequence: You must succeed 3 times in a row. A single failure (chunk, thin, or long) resets the count to zero.
2. Full Swing: The "Big Miss" Elimination
   * HIIT: Plank with alternating leg lifts (60s). Maintain core stability.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The "One-Side" Range. With your driver, the entire left side of the range is out of bounds (or right side if you hook). Hit 10 drives. Your only goal is to eliminate the big miss into the OB zone.
   * Consequence: A single ball in the "OB" zone is a failure. For each failure, perform 5 burpees. Your goal is to get through 10 balls with 2 or fewer failures.
3. Visualization: The Bad Shot Recovery Plan
   * HIIT: Jump Rope (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize hitting a perfect drive, but it takes an awful kick into the trees. Instantly engage your strategic mind. Instead of panicking, visualize assessing your options and choosing the high-percentage punch-out shot that guarantees you get back in play.
   * Consequence: The visualization must be about making the smart, disciplined recovery shot, not the low-percentage hero shot through the trees. Perform this scenario 3 times.
4. Short Game: Controlling Spin
   * HIIT: Medicine Ball Rainbow Slams (30s each side).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The "Two-Hop-and-Stop" Pitch. From 30 yards, hit a pitch shot that takes two bounces and stops quickly. This requires a crisp, accelerating strike. The ball must stop within 10 feet of the hole.
   * Consequence: You must execute 4 out of 6 successful "two-hop-and-stop" shots. Failure requires 10 alternating lunges before restarting.
5. Full Swing: The "Bomber's" Fairway
   * HIIT: Sprint 50 yards, walk back (repeat for 60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Find the two most distant targets on the range and define them as your fairway goalposts. You get 5 drives. You must hit 3 drives that both carry over 250 yards AND finish between the goalposts.
   * Consequence: This is a pass/fail test of distance and control. If you fail, you must re-do the HIIT sprint before starting the 5-drive test again.
6. Visualization: Committing to the Line
   * HIIT: Wall Sit with arm raises (45s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize a tee shot with trouble on both sides of the fairway. Pick your precise, conservative starting line. Your entire visualization is about committing 100% to that line and making an aggressive swing to that safe target.
   * Consequence: Any mental wavering or second-guessing during the visualized swing is a failure. You must execute 3 "fully committed" visualizations in a row.
