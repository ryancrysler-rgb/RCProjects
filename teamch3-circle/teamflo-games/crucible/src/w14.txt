Crucible Blueprint: Week 14 - Playing the Percentages
Mission: To apply your data. This week, every drill is a strategic choice based on the numbers you established. You will learn to swing aggressively to conservative targets11.


1. Short Game: The "Middle of the Green" Putt
   * HIIT: High-Knee Sprints in Place (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Find a long, breaking putt (30+ feet). Your goal is NOT to make it. Your goal is to die the ball in a 3-foot "hoop" around the hole. This trains you to prioritize speed on long putts, eliminating three-putts.
   * Consequence: You must get 4 out of 5 putts to stop inside the hoop. Failure means 10 burpees and restarting the drill.
2. Full Swing: The "Away from the Trouble" Iron
   * HIIT: Box Jumps (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Pick a target green. Imagine there is deep water on the right side. Your target is now the left-center of the green, no matter where the pin is.
   * Consequence: You must hit 5 consecutive iron shots that start left of the pin and land on the green. A single shot that flirts with the "trouble" side resets the count to zero.
3. Visualization: Laying Up with Purpose
   * HIIT: Dumbbell Farmer's Carry (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize a Par 5. You have 220 yards to the green over water. Instead of seeing the green, visualize your favorite full wedge yardage (e.g., 95 yards). Now, visualize hitting a perfect 125-yard iron shot to that exact number, leaving yourself a full swing.
   * Consequence: You must feel that the layup is a strategic, offensive play, not a timid one. Hold this feeling of "strategic confidence" for 60 seconds. Repeat 3 times.
4. Short Game: The "Can't Short-Side It" Pitch
   * HIIT: Lateral Skater Jumps (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From 40 yards, aim at a pin on one side of the green. Your mission is to land the ball on the opposite side of the pin, using the entire green as your target.
   * Consequence: You must succeed on 5 consecutive shots. A single shot that lands on the "short side" of the pin is a failure and resets the count.
5. Full Swing: Driver - The Fairway is Not the Middle
   * HIIT: Kettlebell Swings (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Based on your dispersion data from last week (e.g., a 15-yard fade), your new "fairway" is the left half of the actual fairway. Aim down the left rough line to allow your fade to drift into the short grass.
   * Consequence: You must hit 4 out of 5 drives that land in the designated half of the fairway. This is a pass/fail test of applying your data. Failure requires restarting the drill.
6. Visualization: The "Good Bogey" Game Plan
   * HIIT: Wall Sit (hold until failure).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize hitting a tee shot into a fairway bunker. Your ball is 180 yards from the green. Instead of trying the "hero" shot from the sand, visualize taking a wedge, safely hitting it 100 yards down the fairway, and leaving a comfortable 80-yard wedge to the green.
   * Consequence: You must mentally accept that the goal is now to make a bogey at worst12. The visualization must be calm and strategic. Perform this 3 times.
