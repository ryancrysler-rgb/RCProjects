Crucible Blueprint: Week 11 - Competitive Chaos
Mission: To simulate the chaos and random nature of a real competition. Drills are designed to be unpredictable, forcing you to adapt and execute on command.
1. Short Game: Random Putt Challenge
   * HIIT: Plank Jacks (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Have a partner call out a random distance and break (e.g., "8 feet, left-to-right"). You get one look and one stroke. You must two-putt or better.
   * Consequence: You must succeed on 5 out of 7 random putts. Failure to do so means you perform 10 burpees and restart the 7-putt sequence.
2. Full Swing: The "Play Nine" Simulation
   * HIIT: Max repetition bodyweight squats (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Play 9 "holes" on the range. You must change clubs on every shot (Driver, then 7i, then Wedge, etc.). You must also change your target on every shot.
   * Consequence: Create a scorecard. Your goal is to hit 5 out of 9 "greens" (a designated safe zone). Failure to hit 5 greens means you must perform a 60-second wall sit.
3. Visualization: Adapting to a Different Swing Feel
   * HIIT: Kettlebell Halos (30s each direction).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize arriving at the course with a swing that feels completely "off." Your go-to shot is a fade, but today you are hitting a hard hook. Your drill is to mentally adapt your strategy for the first few holes: aim differently to account for the miss, choose more conservative clubs, and focus on rhythm instead of mechanics.
   * Consequence: The visualization must be about adapting and surviving, not fixing the swing on the course. You must create a clear 3-hole "survival" game plan. Perform this 3 times.
4. Short Game: The "Worst Lie" Scramble
   * HIIT: Mountain Climbers (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Hit 3 chip shots. Have a partner choose the worst of the 3 results. You must get up-and-down from that spot.
   * Consequence: You must succeed 3 out of 4 times. A single failure to get up-and-down requires 10 alternating lunges before the next attempt.
5. Full Swing: The On-Demand Shot Shape
   * HIIT: Lateral Box Jumps (30s each side).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: A partner calls out "draw" or "fade" just before you take your final look at the target. You must execute the correct shot shape and have it land in a 30-yard or three finger  wide fairway.
   * Consequence: You must successfully execute 4 out of 6 on-demand shots. Failure to do so means re-doing the HIIT workout.
6. Visualization: The Mid-Round Reset
   * HIIT: Bicycle Crunches (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize making a double-bogey on the 9th hole to ruin a great front nine. Feel the momentum shift. Your drill is to mentally walk from the 9th green to the 10th tee, using that time to execute a full mental reset. Let go of the last hole, drink water, have a snack, and create a firm, positive game plan for the 10th tee shot.
   * Consequence: You must achieve a fresh, positive mindset by the time you "arrive" at the 10th tee in your visualization. Perform this 3 times.
