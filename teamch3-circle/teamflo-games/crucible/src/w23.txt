Crucible Blueprint: Week 23 - Match Play Mentality
Mission: To develop the aggressive and resilient mindset required for head-to-head competition. Every drill is a direct match against an opponent.
1. Short Game: The "Knock-Out" Putt
   * HIIT: Medicine Ball Slams (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Place one ball 6 feet from the hole. You are 1-down with 1 to play. You must make the putt to tie the match.
   * Consequence: You must make 3 "match-tying" putts in a row. A single miss means you "lose the match" and must re-do the HIIT workout.
2. Full Swing: The "Pressure" Iron
   * HIIT: Rotational Medicine Ball Throws (45s each side).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Your opponent just hit their approach to 15 feet. You are 160 yards out. You must hit the green to stay in the hole.
   * Consequence: You must hit 4 out of 4 greens. Failure to do so means you "lose the hole." The consequence is 10 burpees.
3. Visualization: The Opponent's Mistake
   * HIIT: High-Knee Sprints in Place (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize your opponent hitting their tee shot out-of-bounds. Feel the surge of opportunity. Your drill is to immediately temper that emotion and focus on executing your own conservative game plan: a simple iron or fairway wood to the middle of the fairway.
   * Consequence: You must visualize playing the smart, high-percentage shot, not getting overly aggressive. You must complete this strategic visualization 3 times. Then hit each shot.
4. Short Game: The "Get Up & Down to Win"
   * HIIT: Kettlebell Snatches (45s each side).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: You've missed the green on the final hole of a tied match. Your opponent is in for par. You must get up-and-down to win.
   * Consequence: You get one ball. Pass or Fail. If you fail, you "lose the match." The consequence is to re-do the HIIT workout, then come back and succeed 2 times in a row.
5. Full Swing: The "Aggressive Line" Driver
   * HIIT: Broad Jumps (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: You're on a short Par 4 and need to make a birdie on the last hole. The safe play is an iron, but the aggressive play is a driver over a bunker to leave a short wedge. You must take the aggressive line.
   * Consequence: You must execute the aggressive play and have it finish in a playable position (fairway or light rough) on 3 out of 3 attempts.
