Crucible Blueprint: Week 17 - Building Stability & Low Trajectory
Mission: To create a stable base that can withstand the wind and to master the low, piercing ball flight that neutralizes a headwind.
1. Short Game: The "Stinger" Chip
   * HIIT: Heavy Farmer's Carry (60s). Builds grip strength and total-body stability, anchoring you in the wind.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From 20 yards, you must hit a low, running chip shot that lands short and releases to the hole. No high, floating shots are allowed. The ball must stay below your waist in flight.
   * Consequence: You must get 4 out of 5 chips to finish within a 6-foot circle. A single high-trajectory shot is an automatic failure for that attempt. Failure to meet the 4/5 goal means restarting the drill.
2. Full Swing: The Wind-Cheater Iron
   * HIIT: Kettlebell Goblet Squats (60s). This develops a powerful, stable lower body, your anchor against the wind1.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: With a 6-iron, you must hit 3 consecutive low, "stinger" shots that fly under a designated visual target (e.g., the top of a yardage pole or tree branch). All 3 must land in a 30-yard wide fairway.
   * Consequence: A single shot that flies too high or misses the fairway resets your count to zero.
3. Short Game: Putting on Imperfect Greens (Chipping Green)
   * HIIT: Plank with alternating leg/arm raises (60s). Builds a rock-solid core.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Find the bumpiest, grainiest part of the practice green. You must make 10 consecutive 4-foot putts on this imperfect surface.
   * Consequence: This is a test of a pure, decisive stroke. A single miss resets your count to zero and you perform 5 push-ups.
4. Full Swing: The 3/4 Knockdown
   * HIIT: Prowler Sled Push (45s). Develops the leg drive and grit needed to power through tough conditions.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From 150 yards, you must hit a 3/4 swing "knockdown" shot. The goal is to control trajectory and have it land on the green.
   * Consequence: You must hit 5 out of 5 greens. Failure requires re-doing the HIIT workout before attempting the drill again.
5. Visualization: The Layering Routine
   * HIIT: Jump Rope (90s). Build warmth and endurance for cold rounds.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize playing in 45°F (7°C) weather. Mentally walk through your preparation: putting on layers, keeping your hands warm between shots, and adjusting your club selection for the denser air and restricted swing.
   * Consequence: The visualization must be a proactive plan for managing the cold, not a fantasy of a warm day. You must create a clear 3-step plan for how you'll manage your body and equipment.
