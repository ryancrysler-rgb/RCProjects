Mission: To intentionally seek out uncomfortable situations and perform. Train your mind to be calm and your process to be reliable when it matters most.
1. Short Game: The Unmakable Putt
   * HIIT: Plank Jacks (60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Find a nasty double-breaking 30-foot putt. You get 5 attempts. The goal is NOT to make it. The goal is to have 5 putts where the speed is perfect, leaving a simple tap-in if you miss.
   * Consequence: A single putt that is hit too hard (leaving more than 3 feet) or too soft (more than 3 feet short) is a failure. 10 squat jumps per failure.
2. Full Swing: The Uncomfortable Lie
   * HIIT: Dumbbell Farmer's Carry (45s, heavy weight or most you can safely do)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Take 5 balls and throw them into bad lies: a divot, thick rough, sidehill lie. Your only goal is to execute your full routine and make solid contact, advancing the ball to a playable target.
   * Consequence: A chunked or thinned shot is a failure. You must achieve 4 out of 5 "successful advances." Failure to do so restarts the drill.
3. Visualization: The First Tee Jitters
   * HIIT: Jump Rope (60s, fast pace)
   * "Do or die" Drill: Mentally place yourself on the first tee of a tournament with people watching. Feel the nerves. Your drill is to mentally execute your tactical breathing (e.g., box breathing) until you feel your mental state calm down. Then, execute your visualized pre-shot routine. Then hit a good tee shot.
   * Consequence: You must achieve a state of "calm readiness" 3 consecutive times…or more until you’ve hit three good ones.
4. Short Game: The Must-Make Up & Down
   * HIIT: Wall Sit (hold for 60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The Scrambler's Test. Place your ball in the worst possible spot to a tough pin (e.g., short-sided in a bunker). You have one ball. You must get up and down.
   * Consequence: You must go 3 for 5 from 5 different impossible locations. Failure to get 3 successful up-and-downs requires you to restart the drill from 5 new spots.
5. Full Swing: Shot Shaping on Demand
   * HIIT: Lateral Bounds / Skater Jumps (45s)
   * "Do or die" Drill: Pick a central tee shot target. You must hit a draw, then a fade, then a straight shot. All 3 must land within a 40-yard wide fairway or three fingers wide placing your arm out front of your field of vision.
   * Consequence: Any shot that does not curve as intended or misses the fairway is a failure. You must restart the 3-shot sequence.
6. Visualization: The Closing Stretch
   * HIIT: Squats (60s)
   * "Do or die" Drill: Visualize playing the 16th, 17th, and 18th holes of a tournament where you hold a one-shot lead. See the trouble, acknowledge the pressure, but focus intensely on executing your game plan for each specific hole and target. Play the holes.
Consequence: Your visualization must be strategic and process-focused, not outcome-focused. Perform the full 3-hole stretch mentally without losing focus. Judge yourself. Failure = repeat.
