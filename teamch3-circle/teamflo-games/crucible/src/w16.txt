Crucible Blueprint: Week 16 - Final Exam: The Course Manager
Mission: To synthesize all the strategic principles of the month into a comprehensive decision-making test.
1. Short Game: The Risk/Reward Chip
   * HIIT: Man-Makers (60s). The ultimate test of endurance.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Find a chip to a pin tucked behind a bunker. You have two balls. With the first, you must play the safe shot away from the pin to the fat part of the green. With the second, you can attempt the high-risk "hero shot."
   * Consequence: To pass, your "safe" shot MUST be on the green. The "hero" shot is a bonus. If your safe shot misses the green, you have failed the test. You must succeed 3 times.
2. Full Swing: The Par 5 Decision
   * HIIT: Prowler Sled Push or Car Push (45s).
   * "Do or die" Drill: You've hit a 280-yard drive on a Par 5. You have 240 yards left. You get one shot. You must choose the correct strategic play (go for it or lay up) based on an honest assessment of your skills.
   * Consequence: You pass if you commit to your choice and execute it well (a good lay-up is a pass; a well-struck attempt at the green is a pass). A poorly executed shot because of indecision is a fail. You must pass before moving on.
3. Visualization: The Full Hole Strategy
   * HIIT: Controlled Breathing / Heart Rate Lowering (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Mentally play the hardest Par 4 on your course from start to finish. Verbalize your entire thought process: What's the plan off the tee based on my miss? What's the ideal yardage for my approach? Where is the safe miss on the approach? Where is the pin?
   * Consequence: You must have a clear, logical, and disciplined plan for every shot on the hole15. You must complete the visualization without a single "hopeful" or undisciplined thought.

   4. Short Game: The "Worst Case" Putt
   * HIIT: Wall Sit (hold until failure).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Your approach shot was awful. You now have a 75-foot, triple-breaking putt. Your only goal is to get down in two putts.
   * Consequence: You must successfully two-putt 3 times in a row from 3 different "impossible" locations. A single three-putt resets the count to zero.


   5. Full Swing: The "Protect the Ball Patrick Reed" Tee Shot
   * HIIT: Max Reps Pull-ups or Bodyweight Rows (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: It's the 18th hole and you have a 4-shot lead. The hole has water on the left. A driver brings the water into play. A 4-iron leaves a longer approach but eliminates the water. You MUST choose the 4-iron.
   * Consequence: This is a test of ego suppression. You must hit the 4-iron into the designated safe zone two fingers wide.  Failure to choose and execute with the smart club requires you to restart the entire week's final exam.
