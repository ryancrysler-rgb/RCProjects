Crucible Blueprint: Week 2 - Earning Speed
Ready for more? Welcome to week 2.
Mission: Build and control explosive power. Focus on generating speed while maintaining the control established in Week 1. 
1. Short Game: Lag Putting Control
   * HIIT: Kettlebell or Dumbbell Swings (60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The 3-Foot Circle. From 40 feet away, you have 5 putts. All 5 must come to rest within a 3-foot radius of the cup.
   * Consequence: Any ball outside the circle is a failure. Perform 5 push-ups per failure, then restart the drill.
2. Full Swing: Speed with Control
   * HIIT: Rotational Medicine Ball Slams (45s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The 80/100 Hybrid. With a 7-iron, hit one shot at a controlled 80% effort. Hit the next at 100% max speed. To pass, both shots must land within your 30-yard "fairway channel." You must execute 3 successful pairs.
   * Consequence: If either shot in the pair misses the fairway, the pair is a failure. Restart the pair.
3. Visualization: The Hero Shot Layup
   * HIIT: Box Jumps (60s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize a 230-yard carry over water to a tight pin. Feel the adrenaline. Now, visualize making the disciplined choice: selecting an iron, aiming at a safe lay-up zone, and executing the smart shot.
   * Consequence: Hold the feeling of "smart satisfaction" over "risky temptation" for 30 seconds. Repeat 3 times.
4. Short Game: High & Soft Pitch
   * HIIT: Broad Jumps (45s)
   * "Do or Die" Drill: The Parachute Drop. From 30 yards, place an obstacle (e.g., headcover) between you and the hole on the green. Your pitch shot must carry the obstacle and stop within one flagstick of the hole. You must succeed 4 out of 5 times.
   * Consequence: Failure means a 30-second wall sit, then restart the 5 shots.
5. Full Swing: Driver Power
   * HIIT: Sprint 50 yards, jog back (repeat for 60s)
   * "Do or die" Drill: The Launch Code. You get 3 drives. All 3 must carry a predetermined distance (e.g., 250 yards) AND stay within a 40-yard wide fairway.
   * Consequence: A single failure (either short of the distance or outside the fairway) means you restart the HIIT sprint before re-attempting the 3 drives.
6. Visualization: Recovering from a Bad Drive
   * HIIT: Battle Ropes or Fast Punches (45s)
   * "Do or die" Drill: Visualize hitting your opening tee shot out of bounds. Feel the anger and frustration. Now, mentally execute your post-shot recovery routine: take a deep breath, re-focus, and calmly prepare for your next shot with 100% commitment. Hit it.
   * Consequence: You must successfully transition from the "frustration" state to a "calm and committed" state within 20 seconds. Repeat 3 times.
