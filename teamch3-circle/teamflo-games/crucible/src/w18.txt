Crucible Blueprint: Week 18 - Mastering the Curves
Mission: To learn how to intentionally curve the ball on command. This is a crucial tool for specific pin locations and for holding the ball against the wind.
1. Short Game: The Fade Pitch
   * HIIT: Rotational Medicine Ball Throws (60s). Ingrains the feeling of rotating through the shot.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Set up an alignment stick a few feet in front of you, to the left of the hole. You must hit a low chip that starts left of the stick and spins back towards the hole.
   * Consequence: You must get 3 out of 5 shots to start left and curve right, finishing within 6 feet of the hole. Failure means a 30-second plank before restarting.
2. Full Swing: The Draw Gate
   * HIIT: Dumbbell Single-Arm Rows (45s each side). Builds back strength to control the club path from the inside.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Set up a "gate" with two alignment sticks or headcovers about 4 feet in front of you and slightly to the right of your target line. With a 7-iron, you must swing the club "in-to-out," sending the ball through the gate and drawing it back to the target.
   * Consequence: You must hit 3 consecutive shots through the gate that draw back into a 20-yard wide green. A single push or straight shot resets the count.
3. Visualization: The Right-Tucked Pin
   * HIIT: Kettlebell Halos (60s). Improves shoulder mobility for a better turn and inside path.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize an approach shot to a green with a pin tucked on the right side, guarded by a bunker. Visualize aiming for the center of the green and hitting a controlled fade that curves toward the flag, using the entire green as your margin for error.
   * Consequence: The visualization must be of an aggressive swing to a conservative target line. You must feel in control of the shot shape. Perform this 3 times.
4. Short Game: The Slingshot Pitch
   * HIIT: Lateral Skater Jumps (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From 40 yards, your goal is to hit a low pitch shot that hits the green and releases toward a back pin location.
   * Consequence: You must execute 4 out of 5 shots that successfully land and release  and finish within 6 feet. Shots that spin too much are failures. Failure requires 10 squat jumps.
5. Full Swing: The "Hold-Up" Tee Shot
   * HIIT: Banded Pull-Aparts (60s). Strengthens the upper back to control the clubface.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Imagine a strong left-to-right wind. You must hit a drawing tee shot that starts down the right side of the fairway and curves back against the wind, holding its line.  Opposite if different wind, or lefty.
   * Consequence: You must hit 4 out of 5 drives that start right and curve back into the fairway. A slice or a straight push is a failure.
