Crucible Blueprint: Week 13 - Know Thy Numbers
Mission: To achieve radical clarity on your actual capabilities. We will replace hope with data. This week is about being brutally honest with yourself about your distances and dispersion to build a foundation for smart strategy.  You will be paired with a teammate for the week. 


1. Short Game: Wedge Distance Dial-In
   * HIIT: Kettlebell Goblet Squats (60s). Build lower body stability for precise distance control.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From a flat lie, 10 minutes with your three main wedges (e.g., PW, GW, SW). Use a rangefinder to record the carry distance of each solid shot. This is not a test, it's a data collection mission.
   * Consequence: You must create a "cheat sheet" with your average carry distances for each wedge. Failure to record this data makes the rest of the month useless.
2. Full Swing: Iron Dispersion Test
   * HIIT: Burpees (60s).
   * "Do or die" Drill: With a 7-iron, hit 10 shots to a target. Your goal is not to hit it close, but to honestly assess your shot pattern. Note where every shot lands (short, long, left, right).
   * Consequence: You must identify your today’s "typical miss"2. Is it a fade? A pull? Acknowledge this "brutal fact"3. This self-awareness is non-negotiable for future planning.

   3. Short Game: Worst Ball Chipping
   * HIIT: Alternating Lunges (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: On The Harmon Chipping course at the designated quadrant, you and your partner will hit two chips.  The worst chip will be played out by the other teammate.  

      4. Full Swing: Driver Dispersion Test
      * HIIT: Rotational Medicine Ball Slams (45s each side).
      * "Do or Die" Drill: Hit 10 drivers. Imagine the fairway is three fingers wide. Your goal is not to hit the center, but to record how many you keep "in play."
      * Consequence: Be honest about your miss patterns9. If you have a fade, how many yards is it? If you have a hook, how severe? You must record your findings to build your strategy around them.
