Crucible Blueprint: Week 10 - Mastering the Score Saver
Mission: To develop an elite short game under pressure. This week is dedicated to the shots inside 100 yards that save pars and turn bad rounds into good ones.
1. Short Game: The 30-Foot Putt Race
   * HIIT: Alternating Step-Up Jumps (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: You have two balls and are 45 feet from the hole. You must two-putt with both balls.
   * Consequence: If you three-putt with either ball, you fail. The consequence is to immediately re-do 
   *    * the HIIT workout, then restart the drill. You must succeed 3 consecutive times.
2. Full Swing: The 100-Yard Wedge
   * HIIT: Dumbbell Thrusters (45s). A full-body explosive movement.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From exactly 100 yards, you must hit 3 consecutive shots that finish within a 20-foot circle of the pin.
   * Consequence: A single shot landing outside the circle resets your count to zero.
3. Visualization: The Bogey as a "Win"
   * HIIT: Jump Rope (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize hitting your tee shot into the trees. Your only option is to punch out sideways to the fairway, leaving 150 yards to the green. Mentally, you must reframe your goal for the hole from "par" to "bogey." Visualize hitting the green and two-putting for a "good bogey" that keeps the round from getting worse2.
   * Consequence: You must feel a sense of satisfaction and discipline in making a strategic bogey. Hold this reframed mindset for 30 seconds. Repeat 3 times.
4. Short Game: The Up-and-Down Gauntlet
   * HIIT: Burpees (60s). The ultimate gut check.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: You must successfully get up-and-down from 5 different locations around the green: a tight lie, thick rough, short-sided, a bunker, and a downhill chip.
   * Consequence: You must succeed on 4 out of the 5. If you fail 2 of them, you must perform a 60-second plank before restarting the entire 5-location gauntlet.
5. Full Swing: The "Half-Swing" Punch
   * HIIT: Farmer's Carry (as far as possible in 60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From 120 yards, you can only take a half-swing (9 o'clock to 3 o'clock). You must control the trajectory and distance to land the ball on the green.
   * Consequence: You must land 3 out of 5 shots on the green. Failure requires re-doing the Farmer's Carry before attempting the drill again.
6. Visualization: The Post-Round Analysis
   * HIIT: Deep Breathing / Box Breathing (60s). Lower the heart rate.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize finishing a frustrating round where you scored 5 shots higher than your goal. Instead of anger, mentally walk through a disciplined post-round analysis3. Identify 2 things you did well strategically and 2 poor decisions that cost you strokes.

   * Consequence: The visualization must be objective and analytical, not emotional. You must successfully complete this honest self-assessment 3 times without judgment.
