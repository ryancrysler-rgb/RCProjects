Crucible Blueprint: Week 15 - Adapting to Conditions
Mission: To learn how to adapt your game plan when the course or your swing changes13. A rigid plan breaks; a disciplined strategist adapts.


1. Short Game: The Downhill/Uphill Putt
   * HIIT: Alternating Split Squat Jumps (45s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Find a severe slope. You have one ball. You must lag a 30 foot downhill putt to within 2 feet, then turn around and lag an uphill 30 ft putt to within 2 feet.
   * Consequence: You must successfully execute both putts in the pair. If either putt finishes outside 2 feet, the pair is a failure. You must complete 3 successful pairs.
2. Full Swing: The Wind Cheater
   * HIIT: Medicine Ball Rainbow Slams (45s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Even if there is no wind, you will manufacture it. You must hit three 150-yard shots. The first is a normal trajectory. The second is a low "stinger" into a headwind. The third is a high shot to ride a tailwind.
   * Consequence: A judge (or your honest assessment) must clearly see a difference in the trajectory of all three shots. You must complete 2 successful sets of 3.
3. Visualization: The "Swing of the Day"
   * HIIT: Max Reps Push-ups (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize being on the range and your normal shot shape is gone. You are hitting a 10-yard push-fade today. Mentally go to the first tee. You must adapt your entire game plan for that shot. Visualize aiming down the left side and confidently playing the fade.
   * Consequence: The visualization must be about accepting and planning for your "swing of the day," not fighting it14. You must create a clear, adapted plan. Perform 3 times.
4. Short Game: The Wet/Dry Bunker
   * HIIT: Burpees (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Play two shots from a bunker. For the first, imagine the sand is wet and compact. For the second, imagine it's fluffy (use more bounce and speed). Your goal is to land both on the green.
   * Consequence: You must succeed on 3 consecutive pairs. Failing to get either ball on the green means the pair is a failure and you restart.
5. Full Swing: The Uneven Lie
   * HIIT: Single-Leg Romanian Deadlifts (30s each side). Build balance and stability.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Find a hill. You must hit two solid shots with a 6-iron: one with the ball above your feet, one with the ball below your feet. Both must land in a 40-yard wide safe zone.
   * Consequence: You must complete 3 successful pairs. A chunked, thinned, or wild miss on either shot fails the pair.
