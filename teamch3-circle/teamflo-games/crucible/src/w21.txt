Crucible Blueprint: Week 21 - First Tee to Final Putt
Mission: To simulate the pressure of a full competitive round, from the anxiety of the first tee to the focus required on the final putt.
1. Short Game: The Lag Putt Gauntlet
   * HIIT: Max Repetition Burpees (60s). This spikes your heart rate to simulate the nerves of a critical long putt.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From 50 feet, you must two-putt. Immediately after, you must make an additional 4-foot "comebacker."
   * Consequence: You must succeed 3 consecutive times. A single three-putt or a missed comebacker resets the count to zero and requires a 30-second plank.
2. Full Swing: The "Opening Tee Shot" Fairway
   * HIIT: Explosive Box Jumps or high knee jumps (60s). This builds explosive power and activates your nervous system for the first big swing of the day.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Take your driver. You get one ball. You must hit a two finger wide fairway. This simulates the one-and-done nature of the first tee shot.
   * Consequence: Pass or Fail. If you miss the fairway, you "fail" the opening shot. The consequence is to re-do the HIIT workout before trying again. You must succeed before moving on.
3. Short Game: The Par Saver Challenge
   * HIIT: Squats (60s).
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Miss a practice green on purpose, leaving yourself a 25-yard pitch from the rough. You must get up and down.
   * Consequence: You must succeed 5 out of 5 times. Failure to reach 5 successful up-and-downs means you perform 10 squats and restart the 5-ball sequence.
4. Full Swing: The "Clutch" Iron Shot
   * HIIT: Burpees (20s). This is a gut check for when you're fatigued late in a round.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: From 155 yards, you must hit the green. This simulates the approach shot on the 18th hole when you need a par to win.
   * Consequence: You must hit 4 out of 4 greens. Failure requires a 30 second plank.
5. Visualization: The "Winning Putt" Routine
   * HIIT: Box Breathing (60s). Lower your heart rate and bring focus.
   * "Do or Die" Drill: Visualize having an 8-foot putt on the 18th green to win. The drill is not to see the ball go in, but to visualize executing your pre-putt routine perfectly—reading the green, taking your practice strokes, and committing to your line—without any thought of the outcome.  Then make the putt.
