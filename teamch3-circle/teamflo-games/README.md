# TeamCH3 TeamFLO games and drills

The TeamFLO games and drills from Google Drive (TeamFLO > TeamFLO PlayCalls and its subfolders), rebranded for
TeamCH3 TeamFLO: navy header with the CH3 mark and the Floridian logo, Anton and Barlow Condensed, a lined
Notes and scores area for printing, and a TeamCH3 footer. 8.5 x 11.

- `games.py`: every game's text (78 cards), plus how they group into single sheets and session packs.
- `build.py`: builds every PDF into `pdf/` (single games, session days, bootcamps, drills, FLOmandments and a
  Complete Playbook). Run `python3 build.py` (needs Playwright through node).
- `assets/`: fonts, logos and the three Putting Studio diagrams taken from the originals.

To add or change a game, edit `games.py` and rebuild.

Not rebranded: the Golf Blueprint monthly practice decks (Approach, Distance Control, Driving Specific, Habits and
Process, Intentional Practice/3/Fail, Process 2/3/All Areas/All Aspects, Reflection 1/2, Session 1), which carry
the Golf Blueprint logo; playcall schedules and spreadsheets; the Marina Alex profile; the practice green map.
