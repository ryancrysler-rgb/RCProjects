TEAMCH3 · INVITING A PLAYER AND SIGN-UP
How a new player gets into TeamCH3 (ch3.circle.so), the emails they get, and what they see when they sign up.
Updated October 22, 2026. Everything to paste is between the ----- lines.


THE SHORT VERSION
Now (beta, no payments yet):
 1. You (or Claude) send the player a personal heads-up email. Copy in section 3.
 2. The player is invited into Circle with the right membership. Tell Claude in this chat, or do it in Circle.
 3. Circle sends its invitation email. The player clicks Accept invitation.
 4. They sign up: password, a few questions (date of birth, age group, home city, and a parent's email for juniors).
 5. They land in the Global Basecamp, introduce themselves and see the community rules before their first post.
 6. For a junior, the parent or guardian is invited to the free Parents space by email (section 4).
At launch (payments on):
 The same, except the invite is a checkout link from the membership page: card details and a 14-day free trial
 (no trial for Elite) come first, then steps 4 to 6.


1. CHOOSING THE MEMBERSHIP
Every player goes into exactly one membership (a Circle access group). Their home basecamp is the academy nearest
where they live.
 - Pod Community
 - Junior Basecamp · CH3 Dubai / Junior Basecamp · CH3 Thailand / Junior Basecamp · TeamFLO   (juniors 13 to 19)
 - Premier · CH3 Dubai / Premier · CH3 Thailand / Premier · TeamFLO
 - Elite   (application only. The academy is added by hand)
 - Parents (free, only for the parent or guardian of a junior member)
 - Coaches · CH3 Dubai / Coaches · CH3 Thailand / Coaches · TeamFLO   (free, coaches only. See section 8)
 CH3 Dubai: UAE and Europe. CH3 Thailand: Thailand and Asia. TeamFLO: Florida and North America.


2. INVITING A PLAYER NOW (BETA)
Easiest: tell Claude in this chat
 "Invite [name], [email], [membership]". Claude adds them to Circle and the right access group, and Circle sends
 the invitation email straight away. Claude checks that their spaces are right.

Or in Circle yourself
 Audience > add a member > enter the name and email > choose the access group (the membership) > send the invite.
 Choose only the access group. Don't add spaces or space groups by hand: a space group adds the player to every
 space in it, including ones their membership shouldn't have.

Whoever sends the invite, their name and email appear in Circle's email. Ryan's invites show
rcrysler@floridian.cc. If Claude Harmon invites, the player sees claudeharmon3@gmail.com.

Many players at once (beta testers): see "TeamCH3 - beta tester invite.txt".
Coaches and the academy rollout (Dubai, TeamFLO, Thailand): see section 8.

the player looks for it. Send it from your own email just before the invite.

[Ryan / Claude]

d) After that

4. WHAT THE PLAYER SEES WHEN THEY SIGN UP
 1. They click Accept invitation and land on ch3.circle.so.
 2. They choose a password. Circle may also email them a code to confirm the address (sign-up codes are on).
 3. Sign-up questions, in this order:
    - Date of birth (required): three dropdowns, day, month and year (2013 back to "1964 or earlier"), so no
      impossible date can be entered
    - Age group (required): 13 to 19 · Junior, or 20 or over · Adult
    - Parent or guardian's email (juniors). Juniors can also add it later in Edit profile. Other members never
      see it.
    - Home city (required, kept private): they start typing and pick their city from Circle's place search, so
      typos are corrected (e.g. "Napels FL" finds Naples, Florida). Shown only at sign-up and in Edit profile.
    Each January, add the new youngest year to the year dropdown (e.g. 2014 in 2027). Dropdowns hold at most 50
    options, so drop the oldest year and change "1964 or earlier" to "1965 or earlier" at the same time.
 4. They land in the Global Basecamp, on Claude's pinned welcome post.
 5. The first time they post, the community rules pop up and they agree to them first (web and desktop).
 6. Their sidebar shows only their membership's spaces. On the web, the buttons along the top of the feed jump
    between their basecamps.
 Headline and Bio are no longer on the sign-up form. Members can add them later in Edit profile.

 Parents of juniors
 There is no open Parents link any more: each parent is invited by email, so only a junior's own parent gets in.
  1. The junior adds a parent or guardian's email at sign-up or in Edit profile.
  2. Tell Claude "invite the parents". Claude reads the new juniors' parent emails, invites each parent into the
     Parents membership and fills in "Parent of" with the junior's name. Or in Circle: Audience > add a member >
     the parent's email > the Parents access group.
  3. The parent gets the same Circle invitation email, signs up the same way and lands in the Parents space.
 Parents get Parents, Junior Events and Community Rules only.


5. AT LAUNCH (PAYMENTS ON)
 Once Stripe is connected and the 8 paywalls exist, Claude makes a checkout link for each membership: a Circle
 invitation link with the paywall, the price and the 14-day trial (none for Elite).
 1. The player chooses a membership on the membership page (the buttons already point to these).
 2. Circle checkout: email and card. Nothing is charged until the trial ends.
 3. Circle creates the account and puts them in that membership's access group.
 4. Then sign-up as in section 4, landing in the space the link chooses.
 Juniors: the parent completes checkout with the junior's email and the parent's card, so the account is the
 junior's. Check this with the attorney.
 Elite: no public link. After an application is accepted, Claude makes a one-off Elite checkout link for that
 player.


6. CIRCLE WORKFLOWS
What's there now

 Academies · Premier"): the player picked a region at sign-up, and the workflows added their academy and removed
 it when they left. Since October 14 each academy has its own membership, which adds and removes the right spaces

 what the Elite membership already does. All are off and none has ever run. Leave them off. There's no need to
 archive them.

 Quickest: paste "TeamCH3 - Circle AI build brief.txt" into Circle AI and it builds all 9 with these emails.

 email.
  Trigger: Member added to access group > [the membership's access group]
  Action:  Send email (if your plan doesn't show Send email, use Send DM from Claude's account)

8. COACHES AND THE ACADEMY ROLLOUT
Coach roles
 - Admins: Ryan and Claude Harmon III. Everything.
 - Coaches: every coach is in their academy's Coaches membership, sees all three academy basecamps, and is a space
   moderator of CH3 Dubai, CH3 Thailand, TeamFLO and the Junior Basecamp (decided October 27). A moderator can edit,
   pin and remove posts and manage members in those spaces only. They have no access to community settings,
   analytics, plans or billing.
 - Head coaches: Mike Bolt (CH3 Dubai), Joe Marshall (CH3 Thailand) and Ryan (TeamFLO, already an admin). They have
   the same access as other coaches. They lead their academy's rollout.
 - Every coach gets the public "Coach" member tag, an orange label on their posts, profile and the member list.

What a Coaches membership includes (all three are the same. The academy in the name sets the coach's home academy
and which welcome workflow runs)
 All three academy basecamps (CH3 Dubai, CH3 Thailand, TeamFLO), the Coaches' Basecamp, Junior Basecamp, Junior
 Events, Parents, Global Basecamp, Events, Community Rules, the Son Of A Butch Podcast, Live Recordings and Premier
 Sessions. Not the Elite Basecamp, which stays private to Elite members, Ryan and Claude. It's free and not on the
 membership page.

The Coaches' Basecamp (ch3.circle.so/c/coaches)
 Coaches only, from every academy, with Ryan and Claude. It's for sharing best practice and tackling questions as
 one team, and it sits in its own "Coaches" space group, hidden from everyone else. The topics are Ask the team,
 Best practice, Drills and sessions, and Juniors. Claude's pinned welcome post sets two ground rules: players by
 first name or initials only, with no juniors' personal details, and what's said there stays there. New posts
 send an in-app notification. Email notifications are opt-in.

Inviting coaches (Ryan in Circle, or Claude from a list)
 1. The coach welcome workflows are built and on (Circle AI brief, Part 3).
 2. In Circle, use Audience > add member: one at a time, or a CSV import for the whole academy. Set the defaults to
    the academy's Coaches membership. Don't pick space groups, and don't make anyone an admin.
    Circle sends its invitation email. (Or send Claude the names and emails and Claude does the same through the
    connector.)
 3. Each coach is made a space moderator of CH3 Dubai, CH3 Thailand, TeamFLO and Junior Basecamp, by Circle AI
    (Circle AI brief, Part 4) or by hand: open the space > Members > the coach > Moderator. The connector can't set
    moderators, but Claude can check them.
 4. The coach welcome workflow sends "Welcome to the TeamCH3 coaching team" (Email F in the Circle AI brief, Part 3)
    and adds the Coach tag, about a minute after the coach is added and before they accept the invitation.
    Tested on October 26 with one test coach per academy: both emails arrived and the tag was added.

The rollout, one academy at a time
 Wave 1: CH3 Dubai. Coaches first (13, head coach Mike Bolt). Then Mike sends the Junior Basecamp · CH3 Dubai tester
   link to the academy's families using the parent email in "TeamCH3 - beta tester invite.txt". The families' contact
   details stay with the academy, which keeps UAE and EU data-protection simple. Juniors sign up with a parent's
   email, then Ryan tells Claude "invite the parents".
 Wave 2: TeamFLO. TeamFLO coaches first, then players: adults through the Premier · TeamFLO link, juniors through the
   Junior Basecamp · TeamFLO link.
 Wave 3: CH3 Thailand. As Dubai, with head coach Joe Marshall.
 Before each wave, confirm the academy's coaches have its usual safeguarding checks for working with juniors.
