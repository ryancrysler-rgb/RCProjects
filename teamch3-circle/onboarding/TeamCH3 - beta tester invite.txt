TEAMCH3 · BETA TESTER INVITE
How to bulk-invite player testers so each one picks their own academy and signs up in a couple of minutes.
Created October 22, 2026. Everything to paste is between the ----- lines.

Ready to send (October 23, 2026). A test sign-up got only its own membership, and the welcome email arrived.


HOW IT WORKS
 1. Each tester gets one email with a button for each academy. They click the one nearest where they live.
 2. The link puts them straight into that academy's membership. It's free, with no card and no trial.
 3. They sign up: name, email, password (Circle may email a code), then date of birth, age group and home city.
    Juniors also give a parent's email.
 4. They land in their home basecamp (adults) or the Junior Basecamp (juniors).
 5. Every tester gets the private "Beta tester" tag, so at launch we can find them all in one list. Members never
    see the tag.
 No workflow is needed. The link sets the membership, the academy and the tag.

SENDING IT FROM CIRCLE
 1. Send Claude the testers' names and emails, marked adult or junior. For juniors, send the parent's name and
    email. Claude adds them to Circle as contacts (not members yet) with the "Beta tester" tag. No email goes out
    at this step.
 2. In Circle's email broadcasts, make two broadcasts: "Adult testers" to the adult contacts, and "Junior testers"
    to the parents. Paste the copy below and turn each academy line into a button or link.
 3. Send a test to yourself first, then send.
 If Circle's broadcasts can't be sent to contacts who aren't members yet, send the same text from Gmail with the
 testers in BCC. Claude can make the Gmail draft.

WHILE TESTING
 - Ask Claude "how many testers have joined?" for a count per link and a check that each tester's spaces are right.
 - Ask Claude "invite the parents" after juniors join. Claude invites each parent and checks each junior's age group
   and date of birth, and flags anyone who doesn't look like a junior so you can decide.

WHEN TESTING ENDS
 - Claude switches off the six links. Testers keep their access until you decide.
 - At launch, every tester can be found by the "Beta tester" tag. Move them to paid plans with a checkout link (a
   coupon or a longer trial is possible), or remove them.
 - Delete the test accounts (Test Junior, Test Junior Two, Test Adult, Test Parent, Ryan Tester) before launch.
