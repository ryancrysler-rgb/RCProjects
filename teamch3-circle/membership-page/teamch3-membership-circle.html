FAQ

Questions before you join.

Still not sure? Email rcrysler@floridian.cc and a TeamCH3 admin will help.

REQUEST AN INVITE
Do I need to live near a CH3 academy?

No. TeamCH3 is online. You join the home basecamp closest to where you live (TeamFLO for the Americas, CH3 Dubai for Europe, the Middle East and Africa, CH3 Thailand for Asia and the Pacific) and train with its coaches from wherever you are.

How do I join during the beta?

TeamCH3 is in beta with the CH3 academies, so membership is by invitation for now. Request an invite, or email rcrysler@floridian.cc with your name, home city and the membership you want, and we’ll send it.

What does it cost?

Pod Community is $49 a month, Junior Basecamp $69, Premier $99 and Elite $999. Pay for the year and you get 12 months for the price of 10. Every membership except Elite starts with 2 weeks free.

What’s the difference between Premier and Elite?

Premier is the full basecamp: your home academy, the live webinars, Basecamp Spotlights, Premier Sessions and everything global. Elite adds a private chat with Claude Harmon III and Ryan Crysler. It’s limited to 8 members, by application, with no free trial.

What do juniors get, and how are they kept safe?

Juniors aged 13 to 19 get everything in Premier plus the Junior Basecamp, a juniors-only feed with the CH3 coaches from every academy. Adult members can’t see or join the junior spaces, members can’t send private messages, and a parent or guardian joins the Parents space free.

Do I need a launch monitor or golf data?

No. Post a swing video or tell the coaches about your last round. If you track your rounds or use a launch monitor, you can send that data to RC Greenlight straight from the feed too.

Where do I watch the live sessions?

Inside TeamCH3, in your web browser. RSVP to a session and you’ll get a reminder before it starts.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like, and if you cancel during your 2 free weeks you pay nothing.
